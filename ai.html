How it will work

From PDF to a reviewed draft

A new, optional step between Study Info and Criteria. Skip it and the tool works exactly as it does today.

Upload the study

Add the study PDF and your own AI service key. The full criteria list for the selected study type (in vivo or in vitro) is sent along with the document.

Review side by side

The PDF opens in a large viewer next to the draft. Each criterion shows a proposed score, the reasoning, and a quote with its page. Click a citation to jump straight to that page.

Apply or discard

Nothing touches your assessment until you apply the draft. After that, every score and comment stays editable in the normal Criteria step.

Principles

The evaluator stays in charge

A draft, not a verdict

The AI proposes; the evaluator decides. Scoring rules, mandatory criteria and category thresholds are unchanged from the published ToxRTool methodology.

Every answer is traceable

Each draft score comes with the passage it relies on and where to find it, so it can be verified in seconds rather than taken on trust.

Bring your own key

No account and no server of ours in the middle. The PDF and key go straight from your browser to the AI service you choose to use.

Optional, clearly marked

This is the only feature where data leaves your browser, and only when you start it. Everything else remains fully client-side.
